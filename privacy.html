Privacy policy

This policy covers the personal Google Drive connection described on the home page.

  • It is used only by its owner, with unmodified rclone, to reach the owner's own Google Drive.
  • It touches only the owner's Drive files and file information, in order to upload, download, list and manage them.
  • The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server except Google's.
  • Nothing is sold or given to third parties. The owner shares files only through Google Drive's own sharing settings.
  • Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
Contact: open an issue on the GitHub repository.
